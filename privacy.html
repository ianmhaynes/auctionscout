Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

LotHunters ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use lothunters.com and the LotHunters Chrome extension.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal details to use LotHunters.

When you analyse an auction, the lot data you provide (either via URL, paste, or the browser extension) is sent to the Anthropic API for AI analysis. This data consists solely of publicly available auction catalogue information. It is not stored by us after your session ends.

Chrome Extension

The LotHunters Chrome extension reads auction page content only when you explicitly click "Scrape This Auction." It does not run in the background, track your browsing, or collect any data without your direct action.

Scraped lot data is stored temporarily in your browser's local storage solely to transfer it to lothunters.com. It is deleted immediately after transfer and is never sent to our servers.

Third Party Services

LotHunters uses the Anthropic API to power its AI analysis. Auction data submitted for analysis is processed by Anthropic in accordance with their privacy policy.

LotHunters is hosted on Vercel. Please refer to Vercel's privacy policy for details on their data practices.

Cookies

LotHunters does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@lothunters.com.
